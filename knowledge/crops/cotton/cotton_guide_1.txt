Cotton Cultivation Practices
Climate and Soil
Cotton is a semi-xerophyte. It requires a minimum temperature of 15°C for germination. Optimum temperature for vegetative growth is 21-27°C. It can tolerate temperatures up to 43°C. It requires a frost-free period of 180-200 days. Deep, well-drained, fertile soils with good water holding capacity are ideal. Black cotton soils (Regur) are highly suitable.

Varieties
Selection of varieties should be based on the agro-climatic zone. Short duration varieties are preferred for rainfed conditions to escape terminal drought.

Land Preparation
Deep summer ploughing is recommended to expose resting stages of pests like bollworms and to destroy perennial weeds. Followed by 2-3 harrowings to bring the soil to a fine tilth.

Sowing
Sowing should be taken up with the onset of monsoon in rainfed areas. In irrigated areas, sowing can be pre-poned.
Spacing varies with the variety, soil type, and region (e.g., 90x60 cm or 120x60 cm for hybrids).

Weed Management
Maintain a weed-free environment for the first 60 days. Inter-cultivation and hand weeding are effective.

Harvesting
Picking should be done in the morning hours to avoid dry leaves sticking to the lint. Kapas should be dried in the shade before storage.
