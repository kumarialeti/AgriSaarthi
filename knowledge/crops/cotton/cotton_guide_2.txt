Integrated Pest Management (IPM) in Cotton

Major Pests
1. Sucking pests: Jassids, Aphids, Thrips, Whitefly.
2. Bollworms: American bollworm, Pink bollworm, Spotted bollworm.

IPM Strategies
- Deep summer ploughing to destroy pupae of bollworms.
- Grow trap crops like Marigold, Castor, or Pigeonpea around and within the cotton field to attract bollworms and Spodoptera.
- Avoid late sowing.
- Use yellow sticky traps and pheromone traps for monitoring and mass trapping of pests.
- Encourage natural enemies like Ladybird beetles, Chrysoperla, and Trichogramma egg parasitoids.
- Neem-based formulations can be sprayed as a deterrent.
- Chemical pesticides should only be used as a last resort when the pest population crosses the Economic Threshold Level (ETL).
