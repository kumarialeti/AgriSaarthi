Nutrient Management in Chilli

Apply 25 tonnes/ha FYM as basal. Recommended NPK is 120:60:60 kg/ha for irrigated crops. Apply half N, full P, and half K at transplanting. Remaining N and K in two equal splits at 30 and 60 days after transplanting. Foliar application of micronutrients (Zinc, Boron) improves fruit set and quality.
