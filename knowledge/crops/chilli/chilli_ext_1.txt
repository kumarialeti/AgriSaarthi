Viral Diseases in Chilli

Leaf Curl Virus and Mosaic Viruses are devastating. They are transmitted by vectors like Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci), Thrips, and Aphids. Management: Raise nursery under net house (40-50 mesh). Plant border rows of sorghum/maize as barrier crops. Uproot and destroy infected plants early. Use sticky traps and control vectors using Imidacloprid or Spiromesifen.
