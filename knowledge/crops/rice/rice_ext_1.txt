Integrated Pest Management in Rice

Major Pests:
1. Yellow Stem Borer: Causes 'dead hearts' in vegetative stage and 'whiteheads' in reproductive stage. Management involves clipping seedling tips, using light traps, and applying Cartap Hydrochloride or Chlorantraniliprole at ETL.
2. Brown Planthopper (BPH): Causes hopper burn. Manage by avoiding excess nitrogen, providing alternate wetting and drying, and spraying Pymetrozine or Dinotefuran at ETL.
3. Rice Leaf Folder: Causes folded leaves. Manage using Trichogramma chilonis and spraying Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) 5%.

Major Diseases:
1. Blast: Diamond-shaped lesions. Manage with Tricyclazole.
2. Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB): Yellowish stripes on leaves. Manage using resistant varieties (e.g., Improved Samba Mahsuri) and avoiding excess nitrogen.
