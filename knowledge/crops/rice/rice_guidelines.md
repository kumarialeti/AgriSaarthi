# Rice Crop Production Guide — AgriSaarthi Knowledge Base
# Source: Based on ICAR Rice Production Guidelines (Reference Document)
# Note: This is reference content for agricultural guidance purposes.

## Overview
Rice (Oryza sativa) is the most important food crop in India, especially in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
It is cultivated in Kharif (June-November) and Rabi (November-April) seasons.

## Soil Requirements
- Best soil: Heavy clay loams and silty clay loams with good water retention
- pH range: 5.5 – 7.0 (ideal: 6.0–6.5)
- Minimum depth: 20 cm
- Good water retention capacity essential
- Poor drainage tolerance: moderate

## Climate Requirements
- Temperature: 20–37°C (optimal germination 25–30°C)
- Rainfall: 1000–2000 mm/season
- Humidity: >70% during grain filling
- Requires standing water (flood irrigation) OR precise alternate wetting and drying (AWD)

## Varieties (Andhra Pradesh / Telangana)
- Short duration (100-110 days): MTU 1010, Sambha Mahsuri, JGL 1798
- Medium duration (120-130 days): MTU 7029, BPT 5204 (Samba Mahsuri)
- Long duration (>135 days): Swarna Sub1, Krishnahamsa

## Nutrient Management
General guidelines (always verify with soil test):
- Nitrogen (N): 80–120 kg N/ha depending on yield target and variety
  - Split application: 30% basal + 40% at tillering + 30% at panicle initiation
- Phosphorus (P₂O₅): 40–60 kg/ha (apply as basal dose)
- Potassium (K₂O): 40–60 kg/ha (split: 50% basal + 50% tillering)
- Zinc (ZnSO₄): 25 kg/ha where deficiency is observed
- Organic matter: Apply 5-6 tons/ha of FYM before transplanting

IMPORTANT: Exact doses should be determined by soil test and crop requirement calculations.
Consult local agriculture department or KVK for site-specific recommendations.

## Common Pest & Diseases
1. Brown Plant Hopper (BPH): Yellow patches, hopper burn
   - Monitor: 1 per tiller = action threshold
   - Use light traps; avoid excessive nitrogen
   
2. Leaf Blast (Pyricularia oryzae): Diamond-shaped lesions on leaves
   - Use resistant varieties; seed treatment with Trichoderma
   
3. Bacterial Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae): Water-soaked lesions on leaf margins
   - Use copper-based bactericides; avoid waterlogging
   
4. Sheath Blight: Whitish-gray lesions at water line
   - Improve drainage; avoid dense planting

## Water Management
- Transplanted rice: Maintain 5 cm standing water for first 3 weeks, then alternate wetting/drying
- Direct seeded rice: Irrigation at critical stages (tillering, panicle initiation, grain filling)
- Avoid waterlogging during panicle initiation and ripening

## Harvest Indicators
- 85–90% of grains golden yellow
- Moisture content: 20–22%
- Days after transplanting: varies by variety (100–135 days)
