Disease Management in Groundnut

Major diseases include Tikka (Leaf Spot), Rust, and Collar Rot. For Collar Rot (Aspergillus niger), treat seeds with Tebuconazole or Trichoderma. For Leaf Spot and Rust, spray Chlorothalonil or Hexaconazole at 30, 45, and 60 days if symptoms appear. Crop rotation and field sanitation are critical.
