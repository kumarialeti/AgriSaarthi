Groundnut Agronomy and Cultivation

Climate and Soil
Groundnut requires abundant sunshine and a warm climate. A temperature range of 25-30°C is ideal for growth. It requires a well-distributed rainfall of 500-700 mm during the vegetative and reproductive phases. Well-drained, light sandy loam or loamy soils are best suited, as they facilitate easy peg penetration and pod development. Heavy clay soils are unsuitable because they restrict pod formation and make harvesting difficult.

Seed Treatment
Treat seeds with appropriate bio-fungicides (like Trichoderma viride) to manage seed and soil-borne diseases. Seed inoculation with Rhizobium culture is recommended to enhance nitrogen fixation.

Sowing
Sowing is usually done with the onset of the monsoon (Kharif season) or in the Rabi/Summer season under assured irrigation. Maintain optimal spacing (e.g., 30x10 cm for bunch types).

Nutrient Management
Apply well-rotted Farm Yard Manure (FYM). Gypsum application is crucial during the flowering or pegging stage as it provides calcium and sulphur, which are essential for proper pod filling and increasing oil content.

Irrigation
Critical stages for irrigation are flowering, pegging, and pod development. Moisture stress during these stages drastically reduces yield.
