# Soil Health Guide — AgriSaarthi Knowledge Base
# Source: Reference document based on ICAR, NBSS&LUP soil health card guidelines

## Understanding Soil Test Values

### pH (Soil Reaction)
- <5.5: Highly acidic — aluminum/manganese toxicity possible; lime required
- 5.5–6.0: Moderately acidic — suitable for acid-tolerant crops (groundnut, arhar)
- 6.0–7.0: Optimal for most crops (rice, chilli, cotton, groundnut)
- 7.0–7.5: Neutral — good for most crops
- 7.5–8.5: Moderately alkaline — deficiency of Zn, Fe, Mn common
- >8.5: Highly alkaline (saline-sodic) — gypsum treatment needed

### Nitrogen (N) Status (kg/ha)
- <130 (Low): Heavy yield loss expected; N application essential
- 130–250 (Medium): Moderate N application needed
- >250 (High): Reduce N application; excessive N causes vegetative growth, lodging in rice

### Phosphorus (P₂O₅) Status (kg/ha)
- <10: Very low — full recommended P dose needed
- 10–25: Low — apply 75% recommended dose
- 25–50: Medium — apply 50% recommended dose
- >50: High — minimal or no P application needed

### Potassium (K₂O) Status (kg/ha)
- <100: Low — full K application recommended
- 100–200: Medium — apply 50-75% recommended dose
- >200: High — reduce K fertilizer; excess K competes with Mg, Ca uptake

### Organic Carbon (OC) Percentage
- <0.50%: Low — soil health poor; heavy organic matter inputs needed
  Add: Green manure, FYM (farmyard manure), crop residues, vermicompost
- 0.50–0.75%: Medium — maintain with organic inputs
- >0.75%: High — good soil biological activity; maintain through minimal tillage

### Electrical Conductivity (EC) in dS/m
- <1.0: Non-saline — suitable for all crops
- 1.0–2.0: Slightly saline — sensitive crops (chilli, bean) affected
- 2.0–4.0: Moderately saline — use salt-tolerant varieties (rice, cotton, barley)
- >4.0: Highly saline — leaching + gypsum treatment required

## Common Soil Types (AP/Telangana)

### Red Sandy Loam (Guntur, Prakasam, Krishna)
- Well-drained, low water retention
- Typically: Low N, Low OC, adequate K
- Best for: Chilli, tobacco, groundnut
- Challenge: Moisture stress, low OC

### Black Cotton Soil / Vertisols (Warangal, Kurnool, Mahabubnagar)
- High clay content, cracks when dry
- Typically: Medium-high K, low P, medium N
- Best for: Cotton, sorghum, wheat
- Challenge: Waterlogging in rains; drought stress in summer

### Alluvial Soil (Krishna, Godavari deltas)
- High fertility naturally, good water retention
- Best for: Rice, sugarcane, vegetables
- Challenge: Micronutrient deficiencies (Zn, Fe)

## Soil Health Improvement Strategies
1. Organic matter: Add 5-10 tons/ha of FYM or 2-3 tons of vermicompost
2. Green manure: Dhaincha (Sesbania) — fixes nitrogen
3. Crop rotation: Legume-cereal rotation improves N and OC
4. Reduced tillage: Preserve soil structure and microbial life
5. Mulching: Conserves moisture, adds OC, reduces temperature
