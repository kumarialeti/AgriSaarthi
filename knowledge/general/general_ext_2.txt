Government Schemes for Farmers

1. PM-KISAN: Provides Rs. 6000 per year in three equal installments to eligible landholding farmer families.
2. PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana): Provides insurance cover against crop failure due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases.
3. PKVY (Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana): Promotes organic farming through cluster approach and Participatory Guarantee System (PGS) certification.
